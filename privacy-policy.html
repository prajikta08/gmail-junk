Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Effective immediately upon installation

The short version: Your emails never leave your device or Google's servers. We don't store, sell, or transmit your email content to any third party. Ever.

1. What We Access

MailZen connects to your Gmail account via Google's official OAuth 2.0 system. We request the minimum permissions required:

PermissionWhy we need itWhat we DON'T do
gmail.readonlyRead email headers (sender, date, subject) to identify junkWe never read email bodies
gmail.modifyDelete emails you explicitly choose to removeWe never delete without your confirmation
userinfo.emailDisplay your account name in the extension UIWe never share this with anyone

2. What We Never Do

  • ❌ Read or access email body content
  • ❌ Store your emails or email metadata on any server
  • ❌ Sell, share, or monetize your data in any form
  • ❌ Track you across websites or sessions
  • ❌ Use your data to train AI models
  • ❌ Share data with advertisers or data brokers

3. AI Features & Data Processing

When you use AI Digest, Priority, or Scam Detection features:

Only email subject lines and sender names are sent to the AI. Never email body content. This data is processed transiently and is not stored by our proxy server.

Our proxy server acts as a secure relay between the extension and Anthropic's API. It applies rate limiting and input size limits. No request data is logged or retained after the API call completes.

5. Google OAuth & Permissions

We use Google's OAuth 2.0 flow — you authenticate directly with Google, and we never see your password. You can revoke MailZen's access at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

Our use of Gmail API data complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can see exactly what data is stored locally via Chrome DevTools → Application → Storage
  • Deletion: Sign out from the extension to clear all locally stored data
  • Revocation: Remove Gmail access at any time from your Google Account settings
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the extension removes all local data immediately

7. Changes to This Policy

We'll notify you of material changes via an update notice in the extension. Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance.
